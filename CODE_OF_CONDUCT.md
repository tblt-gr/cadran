# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Cadran Budget welcoming, safe, and respectful for
everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that supports a healthy community include:

- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback with empathy.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and helping repair their impact.
- Keeping discussions focused on the project.
- Respecting other people's privacy and clearly expressed boundaries.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, insults, personal attacks, discriminatory or
sexualized language, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and every space managed
by the project. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct on GitHub, use the platform's private blocking and reporting tools when appropriate.
For a project-specific concern, contact the maintainer through the private channel listed on the
[@tblt-gr GitHub profile](https://github.com/tblt-gr). If none is available, open a discussion asking
for a private contact channel without including sensitive details.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. The
maintainer may edit or remove content, reject a contribution, issue a warning, temporarily limit
participation, or permanently ban someone. Retaliation against a good-faith report is itself a
violation of this Code of Conduct.
